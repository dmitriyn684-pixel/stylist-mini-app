# DimkoFF Visual Brand Guideline 2026 - Edition 02

## Positioning

DimkoFF - SMM + AI Product Builder.

Where audience logic meets product logic.

DimkoFF connects SMM thinking, AI execution and product delivery for founders,
brands and teams that need a path from attention to a working digital system.

## Core formula

SIGNAL -> SYSTEM -> PRODUCT -> GROWTH

- Signal: niche, audience, pain, offer and market energy.
- System: content, funnel, Telegram route and analytics.
- Product: AI bot, Mini App, backend and automation.
- Growth: launch, evidence, retention and iteration.

## Visual principles

- One dominant idea per page.
- Large geometric uppercase typography.
- Dark obsidian foundation with controlled signal mint.
- Warm white for editorial contrast.
- System notes in compact mono typography.
- Selection frames, directional arrows and code-like syntax as active elements.
- Material textures and patterns support the message; they never replace it.
- No acid cyberpunk, gaming effects, decorative clutter or unverified claims.
